Luxury Yachts

Top 10 Luxury Yachts of 2026

Ranked by design, amenities, and global cruising capability

The world of luxury yachting in 2026 is defined by boundary-pushing naval architecture, unprecedented comfort, and an uncompromising pursuit of sustainable cruising technology. From hybrid diesel-electric gigayachts to solar-powered luxury catamarans, today's vessels are floating masterpieces that rival private five-star island resorts. Here is our curated editorial ranking of the top 10 luxury yachts commanding the seas this year.

#1

REV Ocean

LENGTH600 ft (183 m)
BUILDERFincantieri / Vard
GUESTS60 Guests (30 Suites)
RANGE21,000 NM

The REV Ocean is the world's largest and most technologically advanced research and exploration yacht. Designed in collaboration with leading marine scientists and naval architect Espen Øino, it features an oceanographic laboratory, a research submarine capable of diving to 1,000 meters, and a zero-emission electric propulsion mode for navigating sensitive marine sanctuaries. Its aft beach platform transforms into an ocean exploration deck.

#2

Feadship Project 1010

LENGTH390 ft (118 m)
BUILDERFeadship
GUESTS24 Guests (12 Suites)
RANGE6,500 NM

This custom Feadship masterpiece features a striking reverse bow design that improves hydrodynamic fuel efficiency by 12% while providing supreme comfort in challenging seas. The interior architecture embraces organic, flowing geometry with hand-crafted rare woods. The owner's suite commands the entire upper deck with 270-degree floor-to-ceiling panoramic ocean vistas.

#3

Lürssen Opera

LENGTH380 ft (146 m)
BUILDERLürssen
GUESTS28 Guests (14 Suites)
RANGE7,500 NM

Opera represents the zenith of German superyacht engineering. It boasts a dramatic central atrium with a curved glass elevator ascending through all five decks. Amenities include a 20-meter indoor pool with a retractable glass ceiling, two commercial-grade helipads, a hammam spa suite, and a hybrid propulsion plant reducing cruising emissions by 30%.

#4

Benetti B.YOND 48M

LENGTH157 ft (48 m)
BUILDERBenetti
GUESTS14 Guests (7 Suites)
RANGE5,500 NM

Benetti's B.YOND series pioneers the luxury explorer category by fusing steel-hulled transoceanic endurance with haute-couture Italian yacht design. Folding sea terraces transform the beach club into a sprawling private waterfront lounge. Its Siemens hybrid electric propulsion enables 8 hours of whisper-quiet, zero-emission cruising.

#5

Heesen Project Oslo 24

LENGTH213 ft (50 m)
BUILDERHeesen Yachts
GUESTS12 Guests (6 Suites)
RANGE4,800 NM

Project Oslo 24 pairs a patented Fast Displacement aluminum hull with a 28-knot top speed. Styled externally by Omega Architects, the vessel incorporates sustainably harvested FSC-certified teak and lightweight aerospace aluminum. Its glass-bottomed infinity pool on the main deck creates an open waterfall effect visible from the swim platform below.

#6

Amels 80M Limited Editions

LENGTH262 ft (80 m)
BUILDERAmels / Damen Yachting
GUESTS16 Guests (8 Suites)
RANGE5,000 NM

Crafted by Damen Yachting with exterior lines by Espen Øino, the Amels 80M elevates the renowned Limited Editions lineage. It dedicates an entire 2,500 sq ft private deck to the owner's master apartment, complete with secluded jacuzzis and lounging balconies. The sundeck features an open-air starlight cinema and a fully glazed panoramic wellness gym.

#7

Sanlorenzo 62Steel

LENGTH203 ft (62 m)
BUILDERSanlorenzo
GUESTS14 Guests (7 Suites)
RANGE4,500 NM

Sanlorenzo's 62Steel exemplifies refined Italian naval architecture. A four-deck atrium is illuminated by a custom Venetian blown-glass chandelier, while hand-finished Calacatta marble lines the master en-suites. Cruising efficiently at 14 knots with minimal hydro-acoustic noise, it delivers peerless poise across long-range transatlantic crossings.

#8

Holland Jachtbouw Sybaris

LENGTH233 ft (70 m)
BUILDERHolland Jachtbouw / Perini
GUESTS12 Guests (6 Suites)
RANGE6,000 NM

Sybaris represents the pinnacle of modern sailing superyachts. Featuring a plumb bow and carbon-fiber rig designed by Hoek Design, she delivers electrifying performance under sail. Inside, Rhoades Young sculpted an interior rich in Makassar ebony, brushed titanium, and hand-woven silk, complemented by an acoustic Dolby Atmos screening lounge.

#9

Riva 130 Bellissima

LENGTH130 ft (40 m)
BUILDERRiva / Ferretti Group
GUESTS10 Guests (5 Suites)
RANGE2,800 NM

The Bellissima is the undisputed crown jewel of Riva's flybridge fleet. Channeling the dolce vita glamour of the iconic 1960s Aquarama, she marries mirror-gloss mahogany trim with ultramodern carbon-fiber composite construction. A concealed transom hangar houses a custom 6-meter Riva tender alongside jet skis and water toys.

#10

Sunreef 80 Eco

LENGTH80 ft (24 m)
BUILDERSunreef Yachts
GUESTS10 Guests (5 Suites)
RANGEUnlimited (Solar Electric)

The Sunreef 80 Eco proves zero-emission cruising can be profoundly luxurious. Featuring composite-integrated solar skin generating up to 50 kWp, hydro-generation under sail, and advanced ultralight battery packs, she cruises silently with unlimited range in sunny conditions. Her wide 37-foot beam accommodates an expansive open salon.

Final Thoughts

The 2026 luxury yacht market is more diverse and audacious than ever before. While the REV Ocean leads our rankings with its revolutionary fusion of scientific exploration capability and ultra-luxury accommodations, each of these vessels excels in its own domain — from the pure performance of Hoek's Sybaris to the solar-powered autonomy of the Sunreef 80 Eco. As hybrid, hydrogen, and solar technologies mature, superyachts are redefining what is possible at sea.
